Privacy

This site doesn't track you

No analytics, no cookies, no third-party trackers — and the libraries themselves make no network calls either.

What this site does

  • No analytics or tracking scripts. We don't know you visited, and that's fine.
  • No cookies. The site sets a single localStorage key (tf-theme) on your device to remember your light/dark theme choice. It never leaves your browser.
  • Self-hosted fonts and assets. Fonts, styles, and scripts are served from this domain — visiting the site makes no requests to font or CDN providers.
  • One exception: version badges on the product and releases pages are loaded from img.shields.io, which sees a standard image request (your IP address and user agent) when those pages load.

What the libraries do

TerraFluent.Pdf.Reporting, TerraFluent.Html.Reporting, TerraFluent.Docx.Reporting, and TerraFluent.Chart.Reporting collect no telemetry and make no network calls. Your report and chart data stays in your process — output is generated entirely locally.

External services this site links to

Links to GitHub and NuGet take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing on this site sends them data on your behalf before you click.
